\textbf{Internal versus external threats.} The Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR) is the largest published breach dataset; its 2026 edition attributes 88\% of 22,345 breaches with a known actor to external actors and 12\% to internal actors \cite{dbir2026}. Survey studies of insider risk measure cost rather than prevalence \cite{dtex2026}, and IBM finds malicious insiders the costliest initial attack vector rather than the most common \cite{ibm2025}. We return to these sources for RQ2 (a).
